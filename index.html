Mind Dump Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026
Developer: Daynix Studio
Short version: Mind Dump Buddy does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you write stays on your device.

1. Introduction

Mind Dump Buddy ("the App") is a private, offline-first notes application developed by Daynix Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, email address, phone number, or any form of registration to use.

Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact details
  • Your notes, titles, tags, or any content you create
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any form of behavioral or tracking data

3. How Your Notes Are Stored

All notes, tags, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. Your notes never leave your device unless you choose to share them through the Android system share feature (e.g., WhatsApp, Email).

4. Internet & Network Access

The App does not require an internet connection to function. It does not make network requests, upload data, download content, or communicate with any server at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services
  • Cloud backup or sync services
  • Social media integrations beyond the Android share sheet

6. Permissions

The App does not request any sensitive permissions. It uses only standard Android storage access to save your notes in its private app directory, which is managed by the Android operating system and is not accessible to other apps.

7. Data Deletion

Your notes are permanently deleted from your device when:

  • You delete them from the Trash
  • They expire after the 30-day Trash retention period
  • You uninstall the App from your device

Because we do not store your data anywhere else, uninstalling the App removes all your notes permanently.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
